## Best Practices

✅ **Authenticity first**: Choose creators who genuinely align with your brand values and audience

✅ **Creative freedom**: Provide guidelines but allow creators to maintain their authentic voice and style

✅ **Performance incentives**: Include performance-based bonuses to motivate exceptional results

✅ **Long-term thinking**: Focus on building lasting relationships rather than one-off transactions

✅ **Continuous optimization**: Regular analysis and adjustment of creator partnerships based on performance data
