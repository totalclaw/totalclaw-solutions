# Compliance Rules

Use only public, lawful business information.

## Allowed

- official company websites
- public Contact/About/Product pages
- Supplier/Vendor/Wholesale/Distribution pages
- public B2B directories
- public exhibition/exhibitor directories
- industry association pages
- chambers of commerce
- government/company registry pages
- public company social pages
- user-provided files or pasted text

## Not Allowed

- login-required pages
- CAPTCHA bypass
- paywall bypass
- scraping private social profiles
- scraping group members, followers, comments, or friends
- collecting private personal data
- platform restriction circumvention
- fabricated company/contact data

## Social Media

LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X/Twitter, TikTok:

- May be used as public company-page evidence.
- Do not scrape private/person profiles.
- Do not imply personal contact details are verified unless source proves it.

## If User Requests Risky Scraping

Say:

> 这个来源涉及平台限制或个人资料抓取，我不能直接用爬虫抓取。可以改为查询企业官网、公开商业目录、展会名录、B2B平台、政府注册信息，或者分析你已经合法获得并提供的公开文本。

Then continue with compliant alternatives.
