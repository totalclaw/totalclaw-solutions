# Search Keyword Patterns

Use English-first search queries for overseas B2B customer discovery.

## General

- `{product} importer {country}`
- `{product} wholesaler {country}`
- `{product} distributor {country}`
- `{product} buyer {country}`
- `{product} procurement {country}`
- `{product} official website contact {country}`
- `{product} vendor OR supplier OR procurement {country}`
- `{product} wholesale directory {country}`
- `{product} exhibitor OR trade show {country}`
- `{product} contact email WhatsApp {country}`

## Decision Maker

- `{product} "Purchasing Manager" {country} email`
- `{product} "Procurement Manager" {country} WhatsApp`
- `{product} "Sourcing Manager" distributor {country}`
- `{product} "Import Manager" importer {country}`

## Public Company Social Pages

- `site:linkedin.com/company {product} importer {country}`
- `site:facebook.com {product} distributor {country} company`
- `site:instagram.com {product} wholesaler {country} brand`

Use these only as public company evidence. Do not scrape private/personal social data.

## China Sourcing Intent

If the user says "中国采购", "从中国采购", or "source from China":

- `{product} importer source from China {country}`
- `{product} buyer China supplier {country}`
- `{product} distributor China sourcing {country}`
- `{product} private label importer China {country}`

## Image Input

When the user uploads an image:

1. Identify product category.
2. Generate 5-10 English product keywords.
3. Add use-case keywords such as retail, gift, outdoor, hotel, kitchen, pet, construction, industrial, or supermarket.
4. Combine product + use case + country + buyer type.

## Country/Region Tactics

### United States

- Add: importer, distributor, wholesale, retail chain, Amazon seller, promotional products, procurement.
- Useful channels: official sites, Thomasnet, trade show exhibitor pages, industry associations.

### Germany / Europe

- Add: importer, distributor, wholesaler, EU, GmbH, B2B, trade fair, Messe.
- Useful channels: Europages, Kompass, exhibition directories, official distributor websites.

### Middle East

- Add: UAE, Dubai, Saudi, distributor, trading, import, project supplier, hotel supplier.
- Useful channels: chamber listings, trade directories, exhibition pages, official trading company sites.

### Russia / CIS

- Add: importer, distributor, wholesale, официальный сайт, контакты, поставщик, оптом.
- Use English and local-language keywords where useful.

### Latin America

- Add: importador, distribuidor, mayorista, proveedor, contacto, WhatsApp.
- Use English plus local-language keywords.
