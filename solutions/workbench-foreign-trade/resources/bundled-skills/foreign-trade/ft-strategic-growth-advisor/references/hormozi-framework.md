# Alex Hormozi Growth Framework

Based on "$100M Offers" and "$100M Leads" — the most practical frameworks for 
creating irresistible offers and building customer acquisition engines.

## Part 1: The Grand Slam Offer ($100M Offers)

### The Value Equation

This is the most important formula in business:

```
                Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement
Value = ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                    Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice
```

**To increase value, you have four levers:**
1. ↑ Increase the Dream Outcome (make the result bigger/better)
2. ↑ Increase Perceived Likelihood (make them believe it will work)
3. ↓ Decrease Time Delay (make it faster)
4. ↓ Decrease Effort & Sacrifice (make it easier)

### Applying the Value Equation to Physical Products

For e-commerce/physical products, translate the levers:

**Dream Outcome (What does the customer REALLY want?):**
- They don't want a solar mounting bracket — they want a clean energy installation 
  that looks professional, lasts 25 years, and was easy to set up
- They don't want a product — they want the RESULT the product enables
- Ask: "What transformation does my product enable?"

**Perceived Likelihood (Why should they believe this product will work?):**
- Reviews and ratings (social proof)
- Professional product images and A+ content
- Certifications (UL, ETL) — "this won't burn my house down"
- Warranty length and terms
- Detailed specifications and compatibility info
- Video demonstrations
- "Tested to withstand X mph winds / X lbs snow load"

**Time Delay (How fast do they get the result?):**
- Prime shipping / fast delivery
- Pre-assembled vs. requiring assembly
- Clear, step-by-step installation guides
- Tool-free or minimal-tool installation
- "Install in 30 minutes" vs. "professional installation required"

**Effort & Sacrifice (How easy is it?):**
- Plug-and-play design
- Include ALL hardware (no separate trips to hardware store)
- Video installation tutorials
- Customer support via chat/phone
- Money-back guarantee (reduces risk = reduces perceived sacrifice)

### Creating a Grand Slam Offer

**Step 1: Identify the Dream Outcome**
List every problem your ideal customer has related to your product category.

**Step 2: List All Solutions**
For each problem, brainstorm every possible solution.

**Step 3: Trim and Stack**
Remove low-value solutions. Stack high-value ones into a cohesive offer.

**Step 4: Bundle and Name**
Create a named bundle that sounds more valuable than the individual parts.

**Example for Solar Mounting Brackets:**

| Problem | Solution | Delivery |
|---------|----------|----------|
| "I don't know what bracket fits my roof" | Roof type compatibility guide | Free PDF + product selector tool |
| "I'm scared I'll install it wrong" | Step-by-step video series | QR code on package links to playlist |
| "What if it doesn't hold up in storms?" | Wind/snow load test results | Listed in product specs + certificate |
| "I need more than just the bracket" | Complete hardware kit included | All bolts, nuts, flashing included |
| "What if it damages my roof?" | Waterproof flashing + warranty | Included + 10-year warranty |

**Grand Slam Offer Name:** "The Complete Solar Installation Kit — Everything You Need, Zero Guesswork"

### Pricing Strategy (Hormozi's Approach)

**Key principle:** Price is what you pay, value is what you get. If value >> price, 
the offer is irresistible.

**How to charge more:**
1. Bundle products + services + guarantees into one offer
2. Name the bundle (naming creates perceived value)
3. Add bonuses that cost you little but are worth a lot to the customer
4. Offer payment plans for higher-ticket items
5. Create urgency through limited availability (works for B2B especially)

**Pricing Framework:**
```
Your Price should be = Customer's Perceived Value × 0.1 to 0.3

If customer gets $1000 of value → charge $100-300
If customer gets $5000 of value → charge $500-1500
```

## Part 2: Lead Generation ($100M Leads)

### The Core Four: Ways to Get Customers

Every business acquires customers through four channels:

```
                    | You Do It      | Others Do It
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1-to-1 (Outbound)  | Warm Outreach  | Affiliates/Partners
                    | Cold Outreach  | Agencies
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1-to-Many (Content) | Content/Ads   | Customer Referrals
                    | Paid Ads       | Word of Mouth
```

### For Amazon Sellers — The Adapted Core Four

**1. Warm Outreach (1-to-1, you do it):**
- Email past customers with new product launches
- Connect with solar installers on LinkedIn
- Attend industry trade shows (SPI, Intersolar)
- Join solar installer Facebook groups and provide value

**2. Content Marketing (1-to-many, you do it):**
- YouTube: Solar installation tutorials using your products
- Blog/SEO: "How to install solar panels on [roof type]" guides
- TikTok/Instagram: Time-lapse installation videos
- Amazon Posts and Brand Story

**3. Paid Advertising (1-to-many, you pay):**
- Amazon PPC (SP, SB, SD)
- Google Shopping ads
- Facebook/Instagram ads (for DTC site)
- YouTube pre-roll for installation videos

**4. Partners/Affiliates (others do it):**
- Solar installer affiliate program ("recommend our brackets, earn X%")
- YouTube solar channel sponsorships
- Solar DIY community partnerships
- Home improvement bloggers

### The Lead Magnet for Physical Products

Even physical product businesses can use lead magnets:

**Examples:**
- Free "Solar Installation Planning Guide" (PDF, captures email)
- Free "Roof Compatibility Checker" (interactive tool on website)
- Free "Solar Savings Calculator" (drives DTC traffic)
- Free sample/trial units for professional installers

### The Advertising Strategy

**Hormozi's advertising philosophy for growth:**
1. Start with the channel that's most measurable and has fastest feedback (Amazon PPC)
2. Master one channel before adding another
3. Re-invest profits into advertising until you hit diminishing returns
4. Then layer on the next channel
5. Content is the compounding asset — ads are the accelerant

**Recommended channel sequence for Amazon-first solar brands:**
1. Amazon PPC (SP) — master this first
2. Amazon PPC (SB + SD) — expand within Amazon
3. Google Shopping — capture off-Amazon purchase intent
4. YouTube content — build brand + capture research intent
5. Facebook/Instagram — build audience + retarget
6. B2B outreach — direct to installers
