# Risk Control Strategies

This document provides specific risk control recommendations based on customer background check results, including payment methods, shipping strategies, and contract clauses.

---

## I. Risk Level and Response Strategy Overview

| Risk Level | Score Range | Symbol | Overall Strategy |
|------------|-------------|--------|------------------|
| Low Risk | 80-100 | ✅ | Normal cooperation process |
| Medium Risk | 50-79 | ⚠️ | Cautious cooperation, enhanced risk control |
| Unverifiable | 20-49 | ⚠️ | Limited cooperation, strict risk control |
| High Risk | 0-19 | ❌ | Not recommended or extremely strict conditions |

---

## II. Low Risk Customers (Score 80-100) ✅

### Assessment Characteristics

- Company established for long time (>3 years), status active
- Customer is company director/shareholder with complete verification
- Corporate email and phone match company information
- Has customs import records or complete digital assets
- Procurement volume matches company scale

### Payment Method Recommendations

| Method | Recommendation Level | Description |
|--------|---------------------|-------------|
| T/T 30% Advance | ⭐⭐⭐ Recommended | Standard 30% advance, balance before shipment |
| T/T 50% Advance | ⭐⭐ Optional | For large orders |
| Letter of Credit (L/C) | ⭐⭐ Optional | For large orders |
| Open Account (OA) | ⭐ Optional | Only for long-term customers with good credit |

### Shipping Strategy

- ✅ Normal shipping process
- ✅ Partial shipments acceptable
- ✅ Standard quality inspection process

### Contract Clauses

Use standard international trade contract including:
- Quality standards and acceptance clauses
- Delivery time and method
- Dispute resolution mechanism
- Applicable law and arbitration clause

### Special Recommendations

For large orders (>$50,000):
- Recommend purchasing export credit insurance
- Regularly update background check information (annually)

---

## III. Medium Risk Customers (Score 50-79) ⚠️

### Assessment Characteristics

- Company established for shorter time (1-3 years), but status active
- Customer identity partially verified (e.g., LinkedIn confirmed, but no official director record)
- Some information inconsistencies exist
- Procurement volume slightly higher than company scale expectation

### Payment Method Recommendations

| Method | Recommendation Level | Description |
|--------|---------------------|-------------|
| T/T 50% Advance | ⭐⭐⭐ Recommended | Increase advance ratio |
| T/T 100% Advance | ⭐⭐ Optional | For medium amount orders |
| Letter of Credit (L/C) | ⭐⭐⭐ Recommended | Bank guarantee, reduce risk |
| D/P (Documents against Payment) | ⭐ Optional | Requires careful evaluation |

### Shipping Strategy

- ✅ Partial shipment, each batch after payment
- ✅ Recommend sample order verification for first cooperation
- ⚠️ Retain title until full payment received
- ⚠️ Strengthen pre-shipment quality inspection

### Contract Clauses

Add to standard contract:

**Acceptance Clause**:
```
1. Buyer must complete acceptance within 7 working days after receiving goods
2. If no objection raised within specified time, deemed as accepted
3. Acceptance standards based on mutually confirmed sample
```

**Title Retention Clause**:
```
Goods ownership remains with seller until buyer pays in full
If buyer fails to pay on time, seller has right to recover goods
```

### Special Recommendations

- First order amount controlled within $10,000
- After 3+ orders with good payment record, consider adjusting risk level
- Update background check information every 6 months

## IV. Unverifiable Customers (Score 20-49) ⚠️

### Assessment Characteristics

- Company information cannot be verified through official channels
- Customer-company relationship cannot be confirmed
- Using personal email instead of corporate email
- No customs records, digital assets missing
- Multiple verification dimensions using fallback methods still not obtaining valid information

### Payment Method Recommendations

| Method | Recommendation Level | Description |
|--------|---------------------|-------------|
| T/T 100% Advance | ⭐⭐⭐ Required | Must pay 100% in advance |
| Letter of Credit (L/C) | ⭐⭐⭐ Recommended | Irrevocable sight L/C |
| Western Union | ⭐⭐ Optional | Small orders, fast arrival |
| D/P, D/A | ❌ Not Recommended | Too risky |

### Shipping Strategy

- ⚠️ Sample first: Require customer to purchase sample first
- ⚠️ Payment before shipment: Arrange production and shipment after receiving full payment
- ❌ No open account or installment payment accepted
- ⚠️ Cargo tracking: Track logistics status throughout

### Contract Clauses

**Arbitration Clause**:
```
All disputes shall be submitted to [CIETAC]
Arbitration location: [Shenzhen, China]
Arbitration language: Chinese
Arbitration award is final and binding on both parties
```

**Applicable Law Clause**:
```
This contract is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China
```

**Quality Dispute Handling**:
```
In case of quality dispute:
1. Buyer must notify seller in writing within 3 working days after receiving goods
2. Both parties jointly commission third-party inspection agency
3. Inspection costs borne by responsible party
```

### Special Recommendations

- **Strongly recommend** requesting customer to provide:
  - Copy of company registration certificate
  - Director identity proof
  - Latest year's financial statements
  - Bank reference letter

- If customer refuses to provide above materials, recommend declining cooperation

## V. High Risk Customers (Score 0-19) ❌

### Assessment Characteristics

- Company status abnormal (dissolved/in liquidation)
- Fraud signals exist (false information, impersonating company)
- High-risk country/region
- Company very recently established (<6 months) but placing large orders
- Procurement volume severely exceeds company scale
- Refuses to provide any verification information

### Payment Method Recommendations

| Method | Recommendation Level | Description |
|--------|---------------------|-------------|
| T/T 100% Advance | ⭐⭐ Only Option | Must 100% advance, payment before production |
| Letter of Credit | ⭐⭐ Consider | Irrevocable L/C, confirm issuing bank qualification |
| Any credit method | ❌ Prohibited | Absolutely no credit accepted |

### Shipping Strategy

- ❌ Not recommended to ship, unless ALL conditions met:
  1. Full payment received and confirmed
  2. Bank confirms no chargeback risk
  3. Customer provided additional guarantee or deposit

- ⚠️ If must cooperate:
  - Ship in small batches
  - Each batch fully paid before next shipment
  - Video record entire shipping process

### Contract Clauses

**Special Protection Clauses**:

```
1. Advance Payment Clause
   Buyer must pay 100% within 3 working days after signing contract
   If not paid on time, seller has right to cancel order

2. Liquidated Damages Clause
   If buyer cancels order, advance payment non-refundable
   As compensation for seller's production preparation

3. Legal Jurisdiction
   Contract governed by seller's location law
   Disputes submitted to seller's location court or arbitration

4. Guarantee Clause
   Buyer must provide bank guarantee or deposit
```

### Special Recommendations

**Situations Recommended to Decline Cooperation**:

1. ⛔ Company dissolved or in liquidation
2. ⛔ Obvious fraud signals exist
3. ⛔ From sanctioned country/region
4. ⛔ Refuses to provide any identity verification information
5. ⛔ Requests suspicious payment methods

**If Insisting on Cooperation**:

1. Purchase export credit insurance
2. Request customer to provide guarantor
3. Single order amount controlled within $5,000
4. Re-assess risk for each cooperation

## VI. Special Scenario Risk Control Strategies

### 6.1 Large Orders (> $100,000)

Regardless of risk level, for large orders:

1. **Payment Method**:
   - Letter of credit preferred
   - If T/T, require staged payment (30%-40%-30%)

2. **Insurance Measures**:
   - Purchase export credit insurance
   - Confirm buyer's credit limit

3. **Contract Requirements**:
   - Add strict acceptance standards
   - Specify penalties for late payment
   - Add title retention clause

### 6.2 New Customer First Order

Special requirements for first order from new customer:

| Amount Range | Risk Control Measures |
|--------------|----------------------|
| < $5,000 | Standard process, decide based on background check results |
| $5,000-$20,000 | At least 50% advance, recommend sample verification |
| $20,000-$50,000 | At least 70% advance, L/C preferred |
| > $50,000 | 100% advance or L/C, mandatory background check |

### 6.3 High-Risk Countries/Regions

Additional risk control for specific countries/regions:

**Regions Requiring Extra Caution**:
- Countries under international sanctions
- Countries with strict foreign exchange controls
- Countries with unstable political situations

**Risk Control Measures**:
- Must have 100% advance or L/C
- Confirm foreign exchange payment channel is unobstructed
- Consult with export credit insurance company

### 6.4 Abnormal Behavior from Established Customers

For long-term customers showing abnormal behavior:

**Abnormal Signals**:
- Suddenly increasing order amount
- Requesting extended payment terms
- Frequently changing delivery addresses
- Contact person suddenly changed

**Response Measures**:
- Re-execute complete background check
- Contact originally confirmed contact person to verify
- Suspend credit, resume advance payment mode
